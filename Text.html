Demon Slayer

Introduction

Set in Taishō-era Japan, “demons stalk the night and warriors use breathing styles that enhance their ability to slay the demons.” The protagonist, Tanjiro Kamado, is a kind-hearted boy who sells charcoal to support his family. His life changes forever when a vicious demon slaughters his family, leaving only his younger sister, Nezuko, alive. However, Nezuko is infected with demonic blood and transformed into a demon herself. Surprisingly, she retains traces of human emotion and protects Tanjiro, prompting him to become a member of the Demon Slayer Corps to find a cure for his sister and hunt down the demon king, Muzan Kibutsuji.

Story Summary

Training and the Journey BeginTanjiro trains for two years under Sakonji Urokodaki, a retired Water Hashira (elite swordsman), mastering the "Water Breathing" combat style. After surviving the lethal Final Selection exam, he becomes an official Demon Slayer. Accompanied by Nezuko—who travels in a wooden box on his back to avoid sunlight—Tanjiro takes on hazardous missions. In Tokyo's Asakusa district, he encounters Muzan Kibutsuji for the first time and forms an alliance with Tamayo and Yushiro, two benevolent demons working on a cure for demonization.
Expanding Allies and Major BattlesAlong his journey, Tanjiro is joined by two eccentric fellow recruits: Zenitsu Agatsuma: A cowardly fighter who unlocks immense power when unconscious.
Inosuke Hashibira: A wild, boar-masked warrior who uses self-taught beast breathing.

Together, the group fights elite members of Muzan's forces, including the Twelve Kizuki. On a perilous mission aboard the Mugen Train, Tanjiro unlocks the powerful ancestral "Hinokami Kagura" technique with the aid of the Flame Hashira, Kyojuro Rengoku, who tragically falls in battle against the Upper Rank Three demon, Akaza. Subsequent campaigns alongside other Hashira in the Entertainment District and the Swordsmith Village lead to major victories against upper-level demonic threats.
